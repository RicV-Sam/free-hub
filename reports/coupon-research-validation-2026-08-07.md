## Not published

### Aggregator-only evidence

Picodi, WorthEPenny, WikiDeals, SwitchONBenefits, Bountii, CollectOffers, Tiendeo, Catalogue ZA, My Catalogue, Flyers365, Kimbino, CatalogueSpecials, upgradedreviews.com and foroelectricidad.com were not accepted as verification sources. Claims attributed only to these sites were rejected, including the submitted SPAR, Food Lover's Market, OK Foods, Africa Cash & Carry, Take N Pay, Truworths, Matilda's, Tafelberg Furnishers, Shoprite Market Day, Checkers catalogue, KWIKSPAR, Boxer Liquors, OK Furniture and Temu entries.

Restaurant offers attributed only to Restaurants.co.za were not published because the submission did not provide a matching first-party restaurant source. This covers the unnamed sushi/dim sum and teppanyaki deals, Ruby's Cafe, Oude Werf, Vergelegen's Firehorse, Ferryman's Tavern, Beluga and the unnamed three-course offer.

### No current, specific first-party offer

The generic Pick n Pay Smart Shopper, Checkers Xtra Savings, Shoprite, SPAR, Makro, Game, HiFi Corp, Incredible Connection, Russells, Bradlows, Baby City, SHEIN and OneDayOnly descriptions did not identify one stable current offer with complete public terms. Pick n Pay Clothing e-vouchers were face-value payment products rather than a saving. The Amazon `WELCOME15` and BudgetPetSupplies `FURFAM12` codes could not be confirmed on a current official source. The Acer anniversary claim could not be matched to an official Acer South Africa promotion.

### Expired, ineligible or a different content type

- City Lodge `WINTER` ended 31 July; it was replaced by the current official WKND Special.
- The SANParks Wild Card promotion required booking by 7 July.
- Nedbank's submitted voucher and the Beko campaign ended 31 July.
- Vodacom RED's submitted Springbok offer ended 6 August.
- Crossover International, Contempo, Tiger of Sweden, Boxer Liquors and Take N Pay entries were already expired.
- The submitted Woolworths 25% item offer and SPAR voucher competition ended 2 August; Woolworths was replaced with its current official Daily Difference period.
- The Xero Coaches Call offer was for eligible New Zealand customers, not South Africa.
- The Bolt women-owned-business ride allocation was not a general consumer discount and could not be confirmed on Bolt's South African campaign pages.
- OK Foods, SPAR and Sun International prize promotions are competitions or gaming promotions, not coupon/deal records.
- Vintage with Love was an event listing rather than a stated consumer discount.
- Personalised, single-use, app-only or account-targeted codes were not copied into the public registry.

## Review policy

Fast-changing catalogues and app-deal pages received seven-day review windows. Stable loyalty and membership benefits received fourteen-day windows. Cell C is scheduled to stop qualifying after 10 August and Woolworths after 23 August unless a new official period is reviewed and recorded.
