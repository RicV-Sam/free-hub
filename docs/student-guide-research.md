## Editorial maintenance

Review monthly, next due 7 October 2026, and sooner when a provider announces a change. Recheck time-limited offers before their deadlines. No monitor was scheduled. Rechecking means reading current terms and updating the ledger and relevant `lastChecked` field; running the generator must never update evidence dates.

Known deadlines: Gautrain 31 December 2026; Table Mountain student tariff 30 June 2027. The generator intentionally refuses an expired entry or expired highlighted comparison before writing output. Remove or reverify affected content before rebuilding; the existing static live page cannot change until a new deployment succeeds. Review ahead of deadlines to avoid blocking a site build.

Update the page review date after substantive research changes; keep the evergreen URL. Refresh the year in the heading only after the next year's review. Never add hypothetical total savings or fill an empty category with general offers.
