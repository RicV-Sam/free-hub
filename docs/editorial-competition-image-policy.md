# Editorial Competition Image Policy

## Purpose

Every published competition should have a useful, competition-specific visual. Official campaign artwork is preferred when it is accurate, reusable and suitable for the page. When no suitable official hero is available, create a Freehub editorial illustration instead of leaving the listing on a generic category image.

## Source of truth

The approved competition record and the promoter's official source determine what the artwork may claim. Do not infer extra prizes, entry routes, dates, eligibility, product models, quantities or brand partnerships from a generic brief or an earlier draft.

## Production workflow

1. Confirm the competition's title, prize, quantity, entry mechanic, purchase requirement, region and closing date from the approved record.
2. Reuse official campaign artwork only when its claims match those facts and reuse is appropriate.
3. Otherwise, create a landscape editorial hero using the verified facts. Treat logos and branded product depictions conservatively and never imply that Freehub-created artwork is official promoter artwork.
4. Proofread all visible text and compare every factual claim with the source record. Correct or regenerate any image that overstates or changes the offer.
5. Export a web-friendly asset, normally WebP at roughly 1536 × 864, with a sensible quality-to-file-size balance.
6. Add an accurate `imageAlt`, set `imageLayout` to `landscape`, set `imageReviewStatus` to `editorial-generated`, and record the review date and note.
7. Check the page and listing card at desktop and mobile widths before publishing.

## Prompt pattern

Use a tightly constrained prompt: preserve or create the promotional composition, replace every inaccurate claim with the exact verified prize and entry mechanic, remove unsupported imagery, keep supplied correct text unchanged, and produce polished landscape artwork without adding new claims.

## Release checklist

- Prize, quantity and value match the approved record.
- Entry route and purchase requirement are accurate.
- Closing date, geographic limits and eligibility claims are accurate if shown.
- No unsupported product, travel, cash or experience imagery remains.
- Text is legible and free of obvious spelling errors.
- The asset is optimized and the alt text describes the image usefully.
- The page shows the Freehub editorial-artwork disclosure.
- The generated page, metadata image and listing card use the intended asset.
