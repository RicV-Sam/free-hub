## Recommended YouTube videos

### 1. SA Guide-Dogs Suzuki Across raffle

Best long-life video. The car is the strongest instant hook, the safari package adds visual variety, and the Guide-Dogs cause gives the story emotional substance beyond a standard giveaway. Its March 2027 deadline also gives the video months of useful shelf life.

Suggested hook: **Could R150 win you a brand-new Suzuki and a safari for four?**

Video angle: show the car and safari first, explain the R150 ticket and closing times clearly, then close with the work supported by each ticket. Include a visible paid-entry label throughout.

### 2. Wander Out South African conservation safari

Best cinematic short-term video. Cheetah tracking, rhino conservation, Kruger game drives and the wildlife-vet experience create a strong sequence of visual moments, while the US$5,650 prize value gives the title and thumbnail a clear hook.

Suggested hook: **Win 10 days inside South Africa's wildest conservation work**

Video angle: move quickly from Kruger to rhino work, cheetah tracking and the veterinary experience. State the 11 September deadline early and clearly disclose that international flights, visas, insurance and personal spending are excluded.

### Reserve choice

ETR Construction's R100,000 bathroom renovation is the strongest alternative if the channel needs a locally focused transformation story. It offers an obvious before-and-after format but has a very short lead time before 30 August 2026.
