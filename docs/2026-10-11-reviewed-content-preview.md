## What visitors gain

The pages put the actual benefit, eligibility, cost and deadline near the start, then provide practical entry or claim steps and official routes. Store and product tables avoid guessing what qualifies. The coffee comparison helps readers assess their usual spending; the event calendar helps families plan a visit. Birthday and ConnectU additions improve existing guides instead of creating duplicate pages.

The useful facts are in initial HTML, with clear headings, labelled tables, source links, review dates, canonical addresses and relevant structured data. These make the content accessible to readers and machine retrieval; they do not establish indexing, ranking, LLM citation or traffic gains.
